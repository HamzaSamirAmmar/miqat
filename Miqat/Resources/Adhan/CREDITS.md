# Adhan recordings

Bundled high-fidelity adhan recordings sourced and remastered from freely distributed collections
([adhan-audio](https://github.com/mohsalvi/adhan-audio), [adhan-mp3](https://github.com/Kiwifu/adhan-mp3),
and the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org)). All tracks are normalized to EBU R128 (-16 LUFS)
and encoded at 44.1 kHz 128 kbps Joint Stereo for optimal clarity and compact bundle size.
